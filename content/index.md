# Interior Design Knowledge Base

Welcome to my Interior Design Knowledge Base! This website is dedicated to exploring interior decorating, different design styles, color combinations, furniture arrangements, and ways to create beautiful and functional spaces.

Interior decorating has always been something I enjoy because I love seeing how colors, textures, furniture, and small details can completely transform a room. My personal style leans toward modern luxury, but I also enjoy exploring different styles and finding inspiration for unique spaces.

Throughout this knowledge base, I plan to share information about color theory, furniture placement, lighting, decorating techniques, and different interior design trends. My goal is to build a collection of helpful resources and creative ideas while learning more about interior decorating.
